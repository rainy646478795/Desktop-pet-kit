# Cat Photo / Video Guide

## Hard requirements

- Full body visible in every usable shot: head, body, all four legs, tail.
- Tail must be visible and separated enough to read its shape. A tail hidden behind the body is not usable for idle direction frames.
- Cat occupies most of the frame. Keep only enough background for cropping.
- No text, no other pets, no people's faces in the frame.
- No heavy motion blur. Prefer video bursts over blurry stills.

## Shot list

| Shot | Purpose | Checklist |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front stand | idle/waving/look frames | cat faces camera, four feet down, tail visible at one side |
| Side stand | running/idle profile | full side silhouette, legs and tail visible |
| Back stand | look-down/back frames | back fur, tail base, ears from behind |
| Walk video | running-left/right | horizontal side walk, full body in frame, no crop |
| Jump video | jumping | takeoff, airborne, landing moments |
| Paw raise | waving | front paw raised, paw separated from body |
| Close-up head | blink/tilt frames | eyes large, head tilt, face fully visible |

## Filming environment

- Green screen is best; otherwise a plain, single-color background.
- Even lighting, no harsh shadows, no blown highlights on fur.
- 1080p or higher, stable camera, short clips per action.
- For each action, shoot 3-5 seconds so frames can be selected deterministically.

## What to reject

- Head-only selfies
- Cropped paws or tail
- Cat lying down when a standing pose is needed
- Overlapping body parts that make silhouette extraction ambiguous
- Frames where the cat touches the frame edge
